# Support

**Found a bug, have an idea or a question?** Tell us here – every report is read. Bugs and ideas are tracked in the public doDev community repository on GitHub (you need a free GitHub account), or you can simply email us.

## Report a bug

[🐞 Open a bug report](https://github.com/doinittools/doinit-dodev-community/issues/new?template=bug_report.md) – say what you did, what you expected and what happened instead. A screenshot helps.

## Request a feature

[💡 Request a feature](https://github.com/doinittools/doinit-dodev-community/issues/new?template=feature_request.md) – describe the question you want doDev to answer, or the step it would save you.

## Email

[doinittools@gmail.com](mailto:doinittools@gmail.com) – for anything else, or if you'd rather not use GitHub.

## Before you send

> **Please don't include Salesforce data.** Describe the problem and share screenshots **without** customer names, record data, usernames or org IDs.

- **Version:** the doDev version is in the footer of every doDev screen.
- **Answers first:** the [Help](help/) and the [how-to guides](how-to/) cover every tool step by step; the [FAQ](./#faq) answers the common questions.
- **Open issues:** see what others have already reported in the [list of open issues](https://github.com/doinittools/doinit-dodev-community/issues).

## Privacy

doDev runs in your browser and sends nothing to us. Read [how your data is protected](privacy).
