# doDev – Salesforce admin & developer tools in one Chrome extension

doDev is a free Chrome extension for Salesforce admins and developers. It answers the everyday questions about an org – who has access and why, what is different between two users or orgs, and what the data looks like – without installing anything in Salesforce.

**[Install doDev from the Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/agcjfjpmlmfbdbgfohficnccnknimhbd)** · [Help and examples](help/) · [Report a bug or request a feature](https://github.com/doinittools/doinit-dodev-community/issues)

## Who has access in Salesforce – and why

Pick an object, a field, a system permission or a single record and see every user who has access, with the profile, permission set, permission set group or sharing rule that gives it. See who would get access to a record but is blocked by object permissions, and before you change or delete a field, an Apex class or a flow, see everywhere it is used.

## Compare permission sets, profiles, users and orgs

Put two users, profiles or permission sets side by side and see exactly what differs. Compare Apex, flows, objects and org settings between a sandbox and production before a deployment.

## A SOQL query builder – or no SOQL at all

Pick a Salesforce object, check the fields you want, including fields of related records, and run. When you prefer to write SOQL, the editor suggests objects and fields as you type. Update or import records with a preview before anything is saved.

## Safe by design

Everything runs in your browser with the Salesforce login you already have. No server, no account, no connected app, no tracking – and nothing is installed in your org. [Privacy policy](privacy)
